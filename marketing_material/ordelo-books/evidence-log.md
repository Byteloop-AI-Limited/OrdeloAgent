## §1 Platform facts — what WhatsApp can actually carry

- **P1 — Media payloads.** WhatsApp Business (Cloud) API supports images (≤5MB), video (≤16MB), audio incl. voice notes (AAC/MP3/OPUS), and documents (PDF/DOCX etc., ≤100MB). Sufficient for photo receipts, multi-page PDF bank statements, payslips, and spoken expense notes. Sources: [dmly.io file-size guide](https://dmly.io/whatsapp-file-size-guide/), [SleekFlow supported types](https://help.sleekflow.io/en_US/supported-message-types-on-whatsapp-business-api-cloud-a), [Clickatell limits](https://www.clickatell.com/help-center/whatsapp/general-whatsapp/maximum-size-media-message-business-can-send-whatsapp/).
- **P2 — Meta shipped native translation.** WhatsApp launched in-app message translation 23 Sep 2025 — on-device, free, per-chat auto-translate on Android, 19+ languages on iOS. Sources: [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/introducing-message-translations-whatsapp/), [WhatsApp blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/introducing-message-translations), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/23/whatsapp-can-now-translate-messages-on-ios-and-android/).
- **P3 — Reach.** Reused from round 1: WhatsApp is the highest-reach app among UK adults 55+ (63%) (`../drug-commerce/evidence-log.md`). A WhatsApp-integration vendor cites ~98% open rates vs ~20% for professional-services email `[vendor-sourced]`: [ClientWindow blog](https://www.clientwindow.com/resources/blog/stop-wasting-time-chasing-clients-in-mtd).
- **P4 — Chasing mechanics are permitted.** Business-initiated reminders run as pre-approved template messages; client replies open a standard service window. Document/receipt collection for bookkeeping is ordinary business messaging — none of round 1's prescription-drug policy exposure. (Mechanics per the API docs family in P1.)

## §2 The eight problem spaces scanned

### S2 — Mortgage/lending document collection — KILLED (occupied)
Pain is real: brokers describe packaging admin as "relentless", with big chunks of the week chasing payslips/statements; automation vendors claim 12+ hrs/week/adviser recoverable `[vendor-sourced]` ([Adviser IQ](https://www.adviseriq.co.uk/mortgage-broker-workflow-automation)); incomplete documents are the leading cause of application delay ([Boon Brokers](https://boonbrokers.co.uk/mortgage-application-through-broker/)). But **Nivo** owns exactly this: secure messaging + 90-second facial-recognition eIDV, 100+ regulated lenders/building societies/broker networks live, free broker tier since 2020, Mortgage Advice Bureau partnership, and **AI loan-workflow agents launched Apr 2026**. Sources: [UTB partnership](https://www.utbank.co.uk/united-trust-bank-partners-with-fintech-firm-nivo-solutions-to-launch-facial-recognition-id-verification-and-secure-messaging-for-mortgage-customers/), [Nivo security page](https://nivohub.ai/security-and-compliance), [Mortgage Strategy](https://www.mortgagestrategy.co.uk/news/nivo-partners-with-mortgage-advice-bureau-on-instant-messaging/), [Financial Reporter free-tier](https://www.financialreporter.co.uk/nivo-offers-brokers-free-access-to-its-mobile-app-and-messaging-solution.html), [Fintech Global AI agents](https://fintech.global/2026/04/16/nivo-launches-ai-agents-to-streamline-loan-application-workflows/). Verdict: validated demand, occupied by a trusted incumbent that already did the AI-agent pivot.

### S3 — Property maintenance photo triage — KILLED (occupied at the wedge)
Pain and regulatory tailwind are real: letting agents spend ~9.2 hrs/week on tenant maintenance ([Street.co.uk](https://street.co.uk/blog/why-should-letting-agents-use-ai-for-tenant-maintenance-reporting)); a single repair has 20+ touchpoints across tenant/landlord/agent/contractor ([The Negotiator](https://thenegotiator.co.uk/supplier-advice/from-repairs-to-risk-management-the-new-reality-for-letting-agents/)); Awaab's Law fixed repair timeframes bind the social sector from 27 Oct 2025 ([gov.uk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords/awaabs-law-guidance-for-social-landlords-timeframes-for-repairs-in-the-social-rented-sector), [Shelter](https://england.shelter.org.uk/professional_resources/news_and_updates/how_awaabs_law_changes_the_rules_on_hazards_in_social_housing)); agents adopted repair platforms ahead of Renters' Rights Act obligations effective 1 May 2026 ([PropertyWire](https://www.propertywire.com/company-news/letting-agents-adopt-repair-platforms-ahead-of-may-legislation/)). But the WhatsApp-AI wedge is taken: **AskLettie** does 24/7 tenant triage via WhatsApp with photo AI, time-stamped audit trails and one-click legal reports; **Fiixit** covers photo-first repair reporting ([fiixit.co.uk](https://fiixit.co.uk/)). PRS extension of Awaab's Law is explicitly not near-term ([Brabners](https://www.brabners.com/insights/housing/awaabs-law-new-requirements-for-social-landlords-from-october-2025)). Verdict: right thesis, second place.

### S4 — Trades quoting/admin — RUNNER-UP #1
UK Admin Drain Report 2026 (Mar 2026 survey, n=167 owner-operators incl. electricians/plumbers/builders): ~8 hrs/week admin ≈ £17k–£25k+/yr of unbilled time; admin done "at the kitchen table after dinner"; quotes/invoicing top the automation wishlist; headline "ten working weeks a year lost" ([Electrical Times](https://www.electricaltimes.co.uk/uk-tradespeople-losing-ten-working-weeks-a-year-to-admin-they-could-automate-survey-finds/)). Voice-note→quote on WhatsApp is a real multimodal fit. Held back, not killed: crowded job-management app field (Tradify, Powered Now, [TradesMan Quote](https://tradesmanquote.co.uk/) et al.), one-person-business churn economics, and no dated forcing event. Documented in decision log D7.

### S5 — Temp-staffing coordination & compliance docs — RUNNER-UP #2
The sector's own tooling vendors describe agencies running on "spreadsheets, inboxes, WhatsApp messages and paper timesheets", with right-to-work checks, certifications, references and timesheets scattered across systems ([Liveforce](https://liveforce.co/blog/temp-staffing-agency-software/), [Firefish](https://www.firefishsoftware.com/blog/temp-contract-pipeline-tracking-problems), [Tempally](https://www.tempally.co.uk/), [Mobile Rocket](https://mobilerocket.co.uk/compliance-in-temp-recruitment-is-only-a-problem-when-your-processes-are/)). Strong engine fit (shift-fill = dispatch). Held back: digital RTW identity checks must run through certified providers (see S6), the workflow is multi-sided (agency + candidate + client + IDSP), and no fresh dated complaint surfaced in-run. Adjacent to round 2's Ordelo Cover; revisit if Cover discovery calls surface staffing-agency crossover.

### S6 — Standalone ID verification on WhatsApp — KILLED (feature, not company)
Founder-suggested use case. UK digital identity checks for right-to-work/rent/DBS run under DIATF; **34 certified IDSPs** already exist (Yoti, TrustID, OCR Labs, Credas…), with a live government register and a terminology shift to "Digital Verification Services" under way. Certification is the moat and incumbents hold it. Sources: [gov.uk certification guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-identity-certification-for-right-to-work-right-to-rent-and-criminal-record-checks/digital-identity-certification-for-right-to-work-right-to-rent-and-criminal-record-checks), [official register](https://www.digital-identity-services-register.service.gov.uk/), [TrustID on DVS terminology](https://www.trustid.co.uk/blog-uk-digital-identity-framework-the-new-terminology/), [ComplyCube DIATF guide](https://www.complycube.com/en/comprehensive-guide-to-the-uk-diatf-framework/). Verdict: integrate a certified IDSP as a feature inside a vertical agent; never build the IDSP.

### S7 — Insurance claims FNOL via WhatsApp — KILLED (occupied + wrong buyer)
83% of UK insurers are already rolling out AI chatbots/generative AI for claims ([Click4Assistance](https://www.click4assistance.co.uk/web-chat/ai-chatbots/insurance)); WhatsApp FNOL bots with photo upload are commodity vendor offerings ([Floatbot](https://floatbot.ai/automate-insurance-claims-process), [Strada](https://www.getstrada.com/blog/chatbot-insurance)). Enterprise procurement cycles vs a two-person team. Verdict: dead for us.

## §3 Flagship evidence — Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

**The mandate and the clock**
- **M1.** MTD for Income Tax became mandatory **6 April 2026** for sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000; the >£30,000 tier follows April 2027; and (announced March 2025) **>£20,000 from 6 April 2028**. ✓ Verified directly against the HMRC primary in-run: [gov.uk policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-to-sole-traders-and-landlords/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-for-sole-traders-and-landlords).
- **M2.** Populations: **"around 780,000 … will join … from April 2026 with a further 970,000 joining from April 2027"** — ✓ verified verbatim from the same HMRC policy paper; echoed by [AutoEntry](https://www.autoentry.com/news-insights/hmrc-mtd-income-tax-changes-2026).
- **M3.** **First-ever quarterly update is due 7 August 2026** — 27 days from today — covering 6 Apr–5 Jul 2026 (or 1 Apr–30 Jun calendar election). [Keates & Co](https://www.keatesandco.com/making-tax-digital-are-you-ready-for-your-first-quarterly-deadline/), [Sterling & Wells](https://sterlingandwells.com/us/blogs/making-tax-digital-deadlines-2026/), [MTD Explained timeline](https://makingtaxdigitalexplained.com/deadlines-2026).
- **M4.** Digital records mean **every transaction recorded digitally with date, amount and category** in functional compatible software — not annual summaries. [HMRC campaign site](https://makingtaxdigital.campaign.gov.uk/quarterly-updates/), [GoFile knowledgebase](https://gofile.co.uk/knowledgebase/income-tax/keeping-digital-records/), [ICAEW TAXguide 01/25](https://www.icaew.com/technical/tax/tax-faculty/taxguides/2025/taxguide-01-25).
- **M5.** Quarterly updates are **cumulative** — each submission restates year-to-date totals, so earlier-quarter errors are corrected in later submissions (underlying records must also be fixed). ✓ Primary confirms: *"interim cumulative reports must be submitted quarterly on fixed dates"* ([gov.uk policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-to-sole-traders-and-landlords/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-for-sole-traders-and-landlords)); mechanics detail: [Xero MTD ITSA guide](https://www.xero.com/uk/programme/making-tax-digital/mtd-itsa-quarterly-updates/), [HMRC developer service guide](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/guides/income-tax-mtd-end-to-end-service-guide/documentation/make-updates-during-tax-year.html), [ATT FAQs](https://www.att.org.uk/making-tax-digital-technical-faqs).
- **M6.** **Honest counter-fact:** late-submission penalties for quarterly updates are waived for 2026-27; the points regime (£200 at 4 points) bites from 2027-28. The near-term driver is practice production cost, not client fines. [RentalBux deadlines](https://rentalbux.com/blogs/making-tax-digital-key-dates-and-deadlines), [NRLA timeline](https://www.nrla.org.uk/resources/tax/making-tax-digital-timeline-deadline-extension). *(Caveat: rests on two independent secondaries; the LITRG primary fetch failed in-run — recheck before quoting in sales material.)*
- **M13.** HMRC's own compliance-cost estimates for mandated businesses: **~£320 average transitional cost and ~£110 average additional annual cost** (£285/£115 for the >£50k cohort); SA business tax gap ~18.5% (~£5bn); HMRC explicitly anticipates MTD "will have positive impacts on agents", freeing time for complex work. ✓ From the same [gov.uk policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/extension-of-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-to-sole-traders-and-landlords/making-tax-digital-for-income-tax-self-assessment-for-sole-traders-and-landlords) — a state-published willingness-to-pay anchor: the mandate already imposes ~£110/yr per client of accepted new cost.

**The buyer and the pain, dated**
- **M7.** **59% of the 11.2m** self assessment returns filed by 31 Jan 2024 were filed by agents (~6.6m). [AccountingWEB SA statistics](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/tech/accounting-software/self-assessment-statistics-paint-mixed-picture).
- **M8.** Wolters Kluwer research (Apr 2026): **59% of accountants report at least half their income tax clients are still not using digital tools** as MTD goes live. Via [AccountingWEB first-deadline checklist](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/community/industry-insights/mtd-itsa-7-august-2026-deadline-first-quarterly-update-checklist-for).
- **M9.** AccountingWEB industry insight, **published 10 July 2026 — yesterday** — opens: *"your client has just sent a blurry photo of a petrol receipt through WhatsApp"*; warns of inheriting "four rounds of late records" per year; recommends internal cut-offs (~14 July for the 7 Aug deadline); and advises pricing clients with *"mixed banking, manual records, or 'WhatsApp' habits"* at **Premium Fee + Clean-up Phase**. The profession's published answer to WhatsApp-habit clients is to surcharge them, because serving them is unpriced labour. `[vendor-sourced: Receipt Bot sponsored post]` [AccountingWEB](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/community/industry-insights/mtd-filing-will-client-record-keeping-habits-impact-your-profitability).
- **M10.** Glasscubes research: professionals lose **up to 20 hours/month** chasing clients for missing data `[vendor-sourced]`, via [ClientWindow](https://www.clientwindow.com/resources/blog/stop-wasting-time-chasing-clients-in-mtd).
- **M11.** AccountingWEB frames 2026-27 as a **"double workload"**: MTD quarterly cycles running alongside the ordinary Jan 2027 SA season for non-mandated clients. [Operational issues piece](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/community/industry-insights/operational-issues-accountants-face-when-registering-clients-for-mtd).
- **Dv1 (derived).** ~780,000 mandated year-1 × 59% agent representation (M7) ⇒ order of **~460,000 agent-represented mandated clients** in 2026-27. Caveat: SA-wide agent share may differ for the >£50k cohort (plausibly higher); treated as an order-of-magnitude anchor only.

**Incumbent-response watch**
- **M12.** Xero went "all in on AI" at Xerocon London (AccountingWEB, 8 Jul 2026) — the incumbent-AI response is live, priced into risk R1. [AccountingWEB](https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/tech/accounting-software/xero-goes-all-in-on-ai-at-xerocon-london).

**The gap, stated precisely:** a stateful, obligation-aware agent that lives in each client's WhatsApp thread, converts photos/PDFs/voice notes into per-transaction digital records (M4), chases *exactly what's missing* against the practice's cut-off calendar (M9), escalates exceptions to humans, and hands the practice a review-ready cumulative quarter (M5). Every adjacent player validates one face of this; none occupies the intersection. This intersection is precisely Byteloop's existing production pattern: dispatch → accept/verify → escalate → complete, with multimodal payloads.
