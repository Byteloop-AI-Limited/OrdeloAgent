Ordelo turns WhatsApp into Burger Nation's cheapest ordering & delivery channel — you keep 85% of every order instead of ~63–65%.

## Commercial terms (state plainly, no fine print)

1. **15% commission** vs 35–37% at aggregators.
2. **No subscription fee.** One-time £80 setup cost for installing the order printer.

## The two offerings

### Offering 1 — AI-Powered WhatsApp Agent

1. **An additional ordering channel** — doesn't replace anything; adds a direct channel on the app customers already use. No downloads, no accounts, no learning curve.
2. **Not just delivery — takeaways too.** Collection orders flow through the same WhatsApp agent.
3. **Your customers, your data.** Every order builds *Burger Nation's* customer list — names, numbers, order history — not an aggregator's. Re-engage them directly (offers, launch promos, new menu drops).

### Offering 2 — Complete Last-Mile Delivery

1. **Full last-mile delivery** — Ordelo riders pick up and deliver; customer tracks live via a WhatsApp link.
2. **15% commission** — all-in.
