## Why it fits the constraint

One agency = one customer, no marketplace cold start, no CQC-registrable activity on our side (the agent coordinates the agency's own vetted staff), no Meta-policy exposure (workforce coordination is unambiguously permitted), and the dispatch engine maps 1:1 — a care visit is a dispatch with a person-shaped payload. 15,232 registered domiciliary services in England (+81% since 2017) means every borough holds a year of pipeline.

## The honest gap

No primary quote yet from a Bromley agency owner. Weeks 1–3 of the plan are discovery calls with an explicit kill criterion: if uncovered-visit firefighting isn't independently a top-3 pain, this dies cheaply and the wholesale order-capture runner-up gets the same treatment.
