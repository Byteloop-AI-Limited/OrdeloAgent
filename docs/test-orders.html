The ordering customer, restaurant, URL and saved browser preferences do not select test mode. Lowercase, padded or partial names do not match. A marker on a different order has no effect.

The receipt still contains a normal priced item

TEST3100 keeps its configured price, quantity and modifications. It remains in totals, payment items, restaurant messages and paper receipts. Restaurant staff should recognise the marker and must not prepare that order's food.

Keep the payment account fixed

The order and its items are saved together. Their accepted item names determine the stored test/live environment, which remains fixed through checkout retries, capture and cancellation. Existing food-order records remain untouched. No historical reconciliation is required to install the new-order rule.

Keep test deliveries inside overwatch

Overwatch riders must also meet the normal town, vehicle, shift and availability rules. The database filters candidates before ranking and creating dispatch rows. Initial and missed messages recheck the order and current audience. Redispatch after cancellation stays restricted. No eligible overwatch rider means the existing timeout path, never a fallback to ordinary riders.

Install or clean up

If the earlier migration was already run, execute only the first three cleanup statements in the revised file. They drop the readiness helper, optional global payment-mode trigger and its helper. They do not update/delete any food orders or dispatch rows.

For a fresh installation, run the whole revised file, then restart/deploy the updated services. Test fresh ordinary and marker orders. No historical repair or old R1/R3 migration is required.

Cached checkout compatibility remains read-only. Verify Wren checkout/capture and its Stripe webhook separately.
