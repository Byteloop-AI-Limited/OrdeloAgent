# Rider Terms of Engagement

## Delivery Process

1.  **Assignment Notification:** The "Company" will use WhatsApp as the official communication channel to notify Riders of available delivery assignments.

2.  **Assignment Acceptance:** Upon notification, the Rider will have between five (5) to ten (10) seconds to accept the offered assignment.

3.  **Delivery Details:** Following acceptance of an assignment, the Company will provide the Rider with comprehensive delivery details, including:
    *   Compensation amount
    *   Total distance
    *   Pickup time
    *   Pickup location
    *   Drop-off location

4.  **Live Location Tracking:** The Rider must activate live location tracking on their mobile device for the duration of the assignment. Location data (latitude, longitude, and timestamps) will be collected to monitor delivery progress.

5.  **Pickup:** The Rider will proceed to the designated pickup location to collect the item(s) associated with the `order_number`.

6.  **Delivery:** The Rider will transport and deliver the item(s) to the customer at the specified drop-off location.

7.  **Verification Code:** Upon successful completion of the delivery, the customer will provide the Rider with a two-digit verification code. This code is crucial to acknowledge successful delivery.

8.  **Delivery Confirmation:** The Rider will promptly communicate this two-digit verification code to the Company via WhatsApp to confirm successful delivery completion.

9.  **Ending Live Location Tracking:** After the successful delivery confirmation, live location tracking will end. The Rider will receive a WhatsApp message indicating that tracking has ended, OR the Rider may manually close the live tracking app. Location will not be tracked when the Rider is not on an active job.
